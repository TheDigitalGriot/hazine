Their shared story coordinate is a Zustand `progress` value populated from ScrollTrigger's normalized `self.progress`, then sampled by R3F camera/actor callbacks and converted into Theatre sequence position. Starter adds a real Theatre/R3F editable object and custom RAF integration; Hazine currently mounts a core-only sequence-position adapter. ## RAF ownership and coordination boundary

Both Canvas declarations omit a `frameloop` override. Their scene callbacks run inside R3F `useFrame`; neither application source manually calls `gl.render()` or installs a continuous browser `requestAnimationFrame` recursion in the searched `src`. Explicit browser RAFs are one-shot hash/scroll initialization. The starter explicitly feeds Theatre's custom driver from R3F; Hazine has no corresponding custom Theatre driver in source. Anime.js calls and GSAP effects are library-driven transitions; neither source redirects their engine ticker into the R3F loop. Consequently “one normalized story clock” describes the shared story coordinate, not one application-owned wall-clock/RAF scheduler for all four engines. (Hazine `src/components/HazineScene.jsx:70`, `src/App.jsx:113`, `src/scene/TheatreSequenceAdapter.jsx:9`; starter `src/runtime/CanvasRuntime.tsx:43`, `src/motion/ScrollDirector.tsx:19`, `src/theatre/TheatreBridge.tsx:11`; Anime callers cited above.)

Hazine also has decorative motion driven by R3F elapsed time/delta rather than only normalized progress: seal floating uses `clock.elapsedTime`, and ring/core rotation accumulates delta; actor crystal/building rotation accumulates delta while weighted by influence. Its light color/intensity are progress functions. (`src/scene/TreasurySeal.jsx:25`, `src/scene/TreasurySeal.jsx:29`, `src/scene/ActorDirector.jsx:64`, `src/scene/TreasuryLight.jsx:15`.)

## Architectural parity and implementation differences

Parity: scroll owns the story coordinate; Zustand transports it; imperative camera/actor frame callbacks sample it; Theatre position follows it; Anime handles visible DOM/SVG transitions. Both retain a single mounted CameraDirector on their scene entry path. (Hazine `src/components/HazineScene.jsx:31`; starter `src/scene/ReferenceScene.tsx:55`; flow citations above.)

Differences documented in source:

- Theatre integration: Hazine core-only follower / 8-unit empty sequence versus starter editable/provider/custom-driver path / 10-unit empty sequence. (Theatre citations above.)
- Chapter contract: Hazine independent reading-line integer chapters versus starter atomic progress-range/string-chapter resolution with pause/clamp. (`src/App.jsx:40`, `src/state/experience.js:16`; starter `src/state/experience.ts:58`.)
- Knowledge transition input: Hazine independently computes component-local scroll progress from bounding-rect travel and has manual local stage selection; starter follows store selectedLayer driven by chapter or manual selection. (`src/components/KnowledgeTransform.jsx:25`, `src/components/KnowledgeTransform.jsx:57`; starter `src/App.tsx:30`, `src/motion/KnowledgeTransform.tsx:62`.)
- Surface behavior: Hazine story overlay follows chapters 2–6; its separate workbench route uses a 3600ms selected-moment interval, not R3F/GSAP. Starter uses a modal overlay that pauses story progress and owns a separate 2800ms local transcript replay. (`src/components/LiveSystemOverlay.jsx:26`, `src/main.jsx:25`, `src/workbench/WorkbenchApp.jsx:42`; starter `src/components/LiveSystemOverlay.tsx:24`, `src/components/LiveSystemOverlay.tsx:42`.)
- Visual runtime: Hazine builds fog, mineral seal, model actors/globe and gated Sparkles/Bloom/Vignette/Noise in a dark-green scene; starter builds colored procedural actor field, environment lightformers/contact shadows and Sparkles in a dark-violet scene. Those are different scene/rendering compositions, not merely library-version differences. (`src/components/HazineScene.jsx:29`, `src/components/HazineScene.jsx:34`, `src/components/HazineScene.jsx:42`; starter `src/scene/ReferenceScene.tsx:38`, `src/scene/ReferenceScene.tsx:42`, `src/scene/ReferenceScene.tsx:48`, `src/scene/ReferenceScene.tsx:50`.)
